**Analysis of Refined Instagram Video Ad Script with Key Improvements**

---

### **1. Visual Flow Optimization**  
- **Issue:** Abrupt split-screen transitions (e.g., product to benefits) disrupted the viewer's flow.  
- **Improvement:**  
  - Replace abrupt cuts with **gradual, cinematic transitions** (e.g., fade, slow zoom, or seamless scene shifts).  
  - Example: Transition from product showcase to benefits via a **smooth fade** or **slower zoom** to maintain continuity.  
  - Add **subtle animations** (e.g., material textures, cap rotation) to **visualize sustainability** and reinforce "zero-waste" messaging.  
  - **Example:** Use a **gradual reveal** of the bottle’s textures or a **soft spin** on the cap to highlight eco-friendly features.  

---

### **2. Text Overlays for Clarity and Aesthetic**  
- **Issue:** Dense, unspaced text (e.g., "Stainless steel | Bamboo | Biodegradable cap") overwhelmed the viewer.  
- **Improvement:**  
  - **Spacing & Prioritization:** Use **clear, spaced text** (e.g., "Stainless steel | Bamboo | Biodegradable cap" with line breaks) to avoid visual clutter.  
  - **Color Coding:** Use **gradient transitions**
